# Definition of Ready (DoR) and Definition of Done (DoD)

## DoR
- Ticket has `TKT-###` and linked `DEL-###` and `OUT-###`.
- User value and technical scope are clear.
- Acceptance criteria are Given/When/Then.
- Estimate and owner are set.

## DoD
- Acceptance criteria pass.
- Manual test notes are recorded.
- Rollout/rollback notes are captured.
- Relevant docs/boards are updated.

## Outcome completion gate
- All linked deliverables done.
- Outcome success metric reached or explicitly waived in decision log.
