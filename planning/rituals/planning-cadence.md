# Planning Cadence

## Lightweight weekly cycle (solo)
1. **Sprint plan (Mon, 30m):** choose objective and commit <=10 points.
2. **Mid-week check (Wed, 20m):** unblock or cut scope.
3. **Sprint close (Fri, 30m):** demo completed tickets and record slips.

## Sprint boundary routine
- Close sprint doc exit criteria.
- Update risk register and decision log.
- Reorder backlogs by outcome impact.
