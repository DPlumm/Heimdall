## Blocked-item SLA
- 0-4h: log blocker and next step.
- 4-24h: choose workaround or scope cut.
- >24h: move to next highest-value ticket.

## Weekly board hygiene checklist
- Validate outcome linkage for each item.
- Remove tickets without acceptance criteria.
- Re-rank backlog by user value per point.
