## Board standards
- **Workstream statuses:** Discovery, Ready, In Progress, Blocked, Review, Done.
- **Sprint statuses:** Sprint Backlog, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done.
- **Blocked-item SLA:** update blocker note within 4 hours; force replan at 24 hours.
- **Weekly hygiene checklist:**
  1. Ensure each active ticket has OUT/DEL/TKT linkage.
  2. Close stale items with no movement >7 days.
  3. Keep top 5 backlog tickets truly ready.
