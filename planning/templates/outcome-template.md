# Outcome Template

## OUT-### — Outcome title
- **Problem statement:** Describe current user pain in one sentence.
- **Hypothesis:** Describe expected change if this is shipped.
- **Success metric:** Numeric target and unit.
- **Leading indicators:**
  - Early signal 1
  - Early signal 2
- **Owner:** Single accountable owner name.
- **Target sprint:** Sprint number.
- **Linked deliverables:** DEL-### list.
