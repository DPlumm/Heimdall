# MVP Success Metrics

## North-star
Ship a daily-usable MVP that reduces time to inspect events, acknowledge alerts, and produce weekly SLA summaries.

## Outcome metrics
| Outcome | Metric | Baseline (assumed) | MVP target | Cadence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| OUT-001 | Open-to-insight time | 8 min | <=2 min | Weekly |
| OUT-002 | Alert acknowledge time (P75) | 25 min | <=10 min | Weekly |
| OUT-003 | Weekly report prep time | 90 min | <=20 min | Weekly |
| OUT-004 | Repeated setup actions/day | 12 | <=6 | Weekly |

## Leading indicators
- Timeline page load under 2s.
- Alert inbox shows all critical alerts.
- SLA summary generation succeeds daily.
- Preference save/load success >=99%.
